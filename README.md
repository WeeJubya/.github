#WeeJubya#

_Developer | Designer | Creative Tinkerer_

WeeJubya is the creative alter ego behind Grey Door Republik, an independent game studio with a fondness for retro technology, unusual ideas, and worlds that don’t behave quite as expected.

Combining skills in programming, graphic design, animation, audio production, interactive storytelling, and a healthy obsession with the finer details, WeeJubya enjoys bringing all the pieces of a game together, from the underlying code to the smallest atmospheric flourish.

The studio’s debut project, Floppy//144, reflects a particular enthusiasm for technical constraints, environmental storytelling, and making something unexpectedly ambitious out of very little.

Part developer, part designer, part digital archaeologist. 

##Entirely too curious for his own good.##
